- **Fire** — Damage and burning effects

- **Lightning** — Single-target burst damage

- **Evocation** — Offensive, defensive, and utility magic

- **Eldritch** — Ancient and powerful spells (requires manuscripts)

Each school has its own **Focus** material needed to craft related scrolls and gear [citation:13].

### 🖥️ Casting System
- **Spell Scrolls** — Found while exploring, can be cast directly (consumes the scroll)
- **Spellbooks** — Inscribe scrolls into books to build your spell arsenal
- **Staves** — Crafted from wood and unique materials, provide casting bonuses
- **Hotbar Casting** — Equip a spellbook and staff, then cast with right-click
- **Spell Slot Scrolling** — Default: Left Alt to cycle spells

- **Inscription Table** — Inscribe scrolls into spellbooks
