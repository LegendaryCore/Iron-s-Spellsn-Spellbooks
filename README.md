# Iron-s-Spellsn-Spellbooks
Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks - the ultimate RPG spellcasting mod for Minecraft. Click the link and the .jar downloads instantly — no pages, no waiting. 100+ upgradable spells, 9 schools of magic, spellbooks, staves, bosses, and 12 wizard armor sets. Works with Forge and NeoForge on 1.18.2–1.21.1. Requires Iron's Lib, Curios, and Player Animator.
